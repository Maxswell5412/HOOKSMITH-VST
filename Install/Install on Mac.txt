HOOKSMITH by MB Audio - install on a Mac
========================================
Works on Intel and Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) Macs, macOS 11 or newer.
Formats: AU (Ableton, Logic, GarageBand...) and VST3 (Ableton, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig...).

1. Double-click HOOKSMITH-Mac.zip to unzip it.

2. In Finder, click Go > Go to Folder... and paste:
      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   Drag HOOKSMITH.component into that folder.

3. Go > Go to Folder... again and paste:
      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   Drag HOOKSMITH.vst3 into that folder.
   (If a folder doesn't exist yet, create it with File > New Folder.)

4. HOOKSMITH isn't from the App Store, so macOS blocks it until you run this
   once. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this whole
   line and press Return:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/HOOKSMITH.component ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/HOOKSMITH.vst3

5. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.
   Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use Audio Units v2" and
   "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", then click Rescan.
   Logic: it scans by itself on launch.
   HOOKSMITH appears under MB Audio as an instrument.

QUICK START
- Put HOOKSMITH on a MIDI / instrument track and press play.
  You'll hear the built-in sounds right away.
- Pick a Genre, Key, Section (Hook) and Length, then hit GENERATE ALL.
- Each part (Chords, Melody, Bass, Drums) has its own GENERATE and LOCK.
- LOAD on a part puts your own synth on it (Serum, Omnisphere, Vital...).
- GROOVE makes it feel played, COLOR adds tape warmth, TAPE STOP slows it to a halt.
- Drag a DRAG MIDI tile into your DAW to drop the part as a MIDI clip.

If it doesn't show up: run  killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar  in Terminal and rescan.
